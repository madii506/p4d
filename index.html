why

every launchpad is just a config.

on Raydium, a launchpad isn't a company or a server. it's a small on-chain account inside LaunchLab, Raydium's launch program. it holds a name, a website, a logo, a fee and a rule for what happens to the liquidity when a coin graduates. every coin launched through it trades on a LaunchLab curve and graduates into a Raydium pool.

thousands of these platforms already exist. the big ones are just the ones with a crowd. the account itself costs almost nothing to make, anyone can make one, and the owner earns its fee on every trade of every coin launched through it.

p4d makes that one click. pick a name and a look, set your fee and your liquidity rule, sign one transaction, and you have a real launchpad on Raydium with its own page. share the link. when people launch on it, the fees are yours to claim.

how
01

name your pad

pick a name, a logo and a website. they're written into your pad's account on Raydium LaunchLab, so every wallet and explorer that reads LaunchLab sees your brand.

02

set your fee

choose your pad's fee on every curve trade, anywhere from 0% to 5%, plus an optional creator fee up to 0.5%. LaunchLab's own 0.25% protocol fee stays the same on every pad.

03

pick the LP rule

when a coin on your pad fills its curve, it graduates into a Raydium CPMM pool. you choose how much of that LP is burned forever and how much your pad keeps as a Raydium fee key that keeps earning the pool's trading fees.

04

share your link

sign one transaction and your pad is live. you get a launch page at your own p4d link. every coin launched there pays your pad's fee, and you claim it from your dashboard.

faq

questions.

is my pad a real Raydium launchpad?

yes. it's a platform account in Raydium LaunchLab, the same kind every launchpad on LaunchLab uses. coins launched through it trade on LaunchLab's curve and graduate into a Raydium CPMM pool. p4d is independent and isn't affiliated with Raydium.

what does it cost to make one?

just Solana's account rent and network fee for one transaction, paid from your wallet. p4d takes nothing.

how do I earn?

your pad fee is charged on every curve trade of every coin launched through your pad. it collects in your pad's fee vault on LaunchLab, and you claim it to your wallet from the dashboard. if you keep part of the graduation LP, its fee key keeps collecting the Raydium pool's fees too.

what are the limits?

a pad fee from 0% to 5% per trade, a creator fee from 0% to 0.5%, and the LP at graduation split between burned and kept by your pad. LaunchLab doesn't allow giving the creator a share of the LP. one pad per wallet.

can I change my pad later?

LaunchLab lets the pad's admin wallet update the fee wallets, fee rate and LP rule. p4d's editor for that is coming; for now the settings you create with are the ones it keeps.
